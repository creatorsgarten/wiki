:::lead
Notes for organizers running an event at **SCBX NEXT TECH**.
:::

# Internet

- The venue provides Wi-Fi access.
- However, the connection may be unstable or unreliable during events.
  - Mobile internet is recommended for livestreaming or other critical network-dependent tasks.
  - AIS generally provides a stronger signal than Dtac inside the venue.
- Always test the internet connection before the event begins.

# Video

- The venue uses a standard **16:9** display system.
- Standard presentation slides and video output are recommended.
- No special aspect ratio adjustments are required.
- Test the presentation laptop with the venue display before attendees enter the room.
- Bring your own HDMI adapter if using USB-C devices (e.g. MacBook).

# Audio

- The venue provides a digital audio mixer.
- The mixer model may vary between events, so organizers should not assume a specific mixer will always be available.
- Verify the available inputs, outputs, and routing before the event begins.
- If recording or livestreaming, request an XLR or AUX output from the venue's audio engineer whenever possible.
  - Ask the sound engineer to provide a dedicated **recording mix** containing **microphone signals only**.
  - Do not include background music, presentation audio, or system playback in the recording mix unless specifically required.
  - A microphone-only mix provides a clean audio feed for livestreaming, recording, AI transcription, and post-production.
- Perform a complete microphone and playback test before the event starts.

# Livestream

- Test both audio and video at least 60 minutes before the event.
- Verify that the program audio is received correctly by OBS or other streaming software.
- Prepare a backup recording whenever possible.
- Bring spare HDMI cables, USB capture devices, and adapters.

# Presentation

- Presentations should be prepared in **16:9**.
- Use high-contrast slides for better visibility.
- Embed custom fonts or export slides as PDF if font compatibility is a concern.

# Pre-event Checklist

- Verify internet connectivity.
- Test presentation output.
- Test audio from all microphones.
- Verify the recording mix from the audio engineer.
- Confirm that the recording mix contains microphone signals only.
- Verify recording and livestream audio in OBS.
- Check OBS recording and streaming settings.
- Confirm power availability for all equipment.
- Prepare backup internet and HDMI cables.

# Known Issues

- Venue Wi-Fi may become unstable during busy events.
- Mobile internet is generally more reliable for livestreaming.
- The available digital mixer may differ between events, so always verify the setup before the event begins.
